05 / PROFESSIONAL JUDGMENT

Responsible use of data.

I believe it is important to understand what a model can and cannot do.

Privacy by design

Client datasets, private repository links, business records and credentials are excluded. The inventory demo uses fictional data. The published project visuals are selected outputs, not original data files.

Evaluate honestly

Training performance is not proof of future generalization. I report the recorded evaluation results, and identify possible leakage, data quality problems and limited sample sizes.

Understand the data

Missing values, duplicate records and sampling bias can affect conclusions. I distinguish a recorded result from a proposed improvement and avoid claiming that a model is production-ready without evidence.
